# New Forms Implementation Plan (stage 5 of 5)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** This plan adds the 41 new forms (5 children, 8 young, 28 adults) to the game.
- Evolution reads four new inputs, as spec chapter 9 lays out: the room the pet grew up in, the kinds of furniture placed there, what it wears, and care given in the morning.
- The zukan is laid out by family.
- The 6 new egg patterns join the rotation.
- The weak spots found in the 2026-11-06 art are fixed first.

**Architecture:**
- **Inputs for evolution.**
  - `pet.history` gains three per-stage counts: minutes spent in each room, the care count, and the morning care count. They reset at each evolution, the same as `foodCounts`.
  - The engine passes an `env` into `advanceTime` for evolution to read: the current room, the placed decor of the room the pet grew up in, and the worn items.
  - The engine also counts morning care at the moment care is accepted.
- **Evolution.** `EvolutionCondition` gains `room`, `decorKind`, `wearing` and `morningCare`. `matches()` reads them from an `EvolutionEnv`.
- **Data.**
  - 41 `FormDef`s, with priorities and `from` lists as spec 9 defines them.
  - `FORM_GAITS`, `FORM_ANCHORS` (including `squashRow`), names and hints.
  - The data tests already force a gait, anchors and art for every form.
- **Zukan.** It is grouped by family. Spec 10 sets the order.

**Tech Stack:** Preact, TypeScript, Zod 4, Vitest + @testing-library/preact (happy-dom), Playwright e2e, PixelLab MCP, pngjs art scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-home-shop-and-new-forms-design.md`. This plan covers:
- chapter 3 (stage 5)
- 6.3 and 6.4 (the room the pet grew up in)
- 7.3 (decor kinds as an input)
- 8.4 (accessories as an input)
- chapter 9 (all of it, including the owner's choice B for morning care)
- chapter 10 (the zukan)
- the stage-5 parts of 12, 13 (art fixes, eggs) and 14
- appendix A

**Builds on stages 1–4, merged to main (5d1bbf9):**

  - It is the room with the most awake minutes in this stage.

  - The egg stage counts nothing.
- **Decor kind (spec 7.3).**
  - Count the kinds of the items placed in that room (`placedIn` + `decorDef(id).kind`).

  - A tie means no kind.
- **Morning care (spec 9.4, owner choice B).**
  - Morning is the 3 hours from `settings.sleepEndMin`, local time, wrapping past midnight.
  - The care counted is `ok` results of `feed`, `play`, `clean` and `medicine`. `pet` and `lights` don't count.
  - It matches when this stage's care count is ≥ 10 **and** morning care × 2 ≥ care count.
  - The counts live in `pet.history` and reset at evolution.
- **Wearing (spec 8.4).** It matches when `home.worn[place]` is the item at the moment of evolution.
- **Hints (spec 9.6).** Every new form has `hint_<id>` in ja and en, written in the existing style, which hints at the condition. Examples:

## Review Focus

1. **A player who never touches rooms, decor or accessories.**
   - Expected: evolution gives exactly what it gave before stage 5. New branches only win when their input is present, except the morning-care adults. Those take a share of evolutions from the existing catch, cups, stop and default adults when the player cares mostly in the morning.
   - Tested in Task 3 (`the old outcomes are unchanged without the new inputs`).
2. **An old save mid-stage.**
   - The save has no room minutes and no care counts.
   - Expected: it loads. The room it grew up in falls back to the current room. Morning care cannot match until 10 cares are counted.
   - Tested in Task 2 (`a history without the new counts loads with zeros`).
3. **Morning windows across midnight.**
   - `sleepEndMin` is 23:00, so morning runs 23:00 to 02:00.
   - Expected: care at 00:30 counts as morning, and care at 02:00 does not.
   - Tested in Task 2 (`morning wraps past midnight`).
4. **Ties.**
   - Two rooms with equal minutes. Two decor kinds with equal counts.
   - Expected: the room falls back to the current room. The decor kind gives no match, and the next priority wins.
   - Tested in Task 2 and Task 3.
5. **Every new form reachable, and every form still has a default path.**
   - Expected: for each of the 41, a seeded pet with the stated care style evolves into it.
   - Expected: every non-adult form keeps exactly one `{}` fallback.
   - Tested in Task 4 (the table-driven `each new form is reached by its care style` and the existing data invariants).

### Task 3: The new evolution conditions, and the engine's env

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `EvolutionCondition` gains `room?: RoomId`, `decorKind?: DecorKind`, `wearing?: AccessoryId` and `morningCare?: true`.
  - `interface EvolutionEnv { room: RoomId; decorIn(room: RoomId): readonly DecorId[]; worn: Partial<Record<WearPlace, AccessoryId>>; }`. `room` is the current shown room.
  - `EvolutionContext` gains `env: EvolutionEnv`.
  - `matches()` checks:
    - `room`: equals `grownRoom(history, env.room)`.
    - `decorKind`: equals the unique most common non-null kind among `env.decorIn(grownRoom(...))`.
    - `wearing`: equals the worn item in that accessory's place.
    - `morningCare`: `history.care >= 10 && history.morningCare * 2 >= history.care`.
    - `chance` is still evaluated last.
  - The engine:
    - builds the env from `loaded.home` and `loaded.progress.claimed`, using `shownRoom` and `placedIn`, and passes it to `advanceTime`;
    - after an `ok` feed, play, clean or medicine, applies `countCare` with the current local minute and `settings.sleepEndMin` to `save.game.pet.history`.
  - Messages: none.
