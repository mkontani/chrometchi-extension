# Dress-up Implementation Plan (stage 4 of 5)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The player buys accessories in a new アクセサリー tab in おみせ. They dress the pet from a new きせかえ tab in もようがえ, with one item each on the head, the face and the neck. Worn items are drawn on the pet in the popup and move with it. This stage also adds the economy balance test that stage 3 deferred.

**Architecture:**
- **Data and rules.**
  - `src/data/accessories.ts` lists the 25 items: where each is worn, its price, its size, and its own anchor point in its art.
  - `src/data/anchors.ts` gives every form that can wear things (everything except the egg) a head, face and neck point. Points use 32 px art coordinates.
  - `src/core/dressup.ts` holds `buyAccessory`, `wear` and `takeOffAll`. They are pure and return outcomes, like stage 3's `buyDecor` and `placeDecor`.
- **Save.** `home` gains `ownedAcc` and `worn`. The never-failing `parseHome` cleans both, the same way stage 3 handles furniture.
- **Engine.** A new egg (restart or startNewEgg) takes everything off.
- **Popup.**
  - The stage draws the pet and its worn items inside one wrapper, so the idle bounce and the care-effect slide move them together.
  - おみせ gains a third tab. もようがえ gains a third tab.
  - The bought note says what was bought.

**Tech Stack:** Preact, TypeScript, Zod 4, Vitest + @testing-library/preact (happy-dom), Playwright e2e, PixelLab MCP, pngjs art scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-home-shop-and-new-forms-design.md`. This plan covers:
- chapter 3 (stage 4)
- chapter 8 (except 8.4, which is evolution and belongs to stage 5)
- the アクセサリー part of 11.3 and the きせかえ part of 11.2
- the stage-4 parts of 12 and 13
- appendix C
- spec 5.2's balance target, moved here by stage 3's ruling

## Global Constraints

- **Items (spec appendix C).**
  - The format is `id: name ja / en — place — price — art size`.
  - Names lean on kana, following stage 3's ruling.
  - **Head:**

    - strawhat: むぎわらぼうし / Straw hat — head — 80 — 20
    - flowercrown: はなかんむり / Flower crown — head — 150 — 24
    - chefhat: コックぼうし / Chef hat — head — 100 — 16
    - beanie: ニットぼう / Beanie — head — 80 — 16
    - santahat: サンタぼうし / Santa hat — head — 120 — 16
    - tiara: ティアラ / Tiara — head — 200 — 16
    - cap: キャップ / Cap — head — 60 — 16
    - wizardhat: まほうつかいのぼうし / Wizard hat — head — 150 — 20
    - headphones: ヘッドホン / Headphones — head — 150 — 20
    - flowerpin: はなのヘアピン / Flower pin — head — 50 — 16
    - starclip: ほしのヘアピン / Star clip — head — 50 — 16
    - leaf: はっぱ / Leaf — head — 50 — 16
  - **Face:**
    - glasses: まるめがね / Round glasses — face — 80 — 16
    - sunglasses: サングラス / Sunglasses — face — 100 — 16
    - heartglasses: ハートのサングラス / Heart glasses — face — 120 — 16
  - **Neck:**
    - bowtie: ちょうネクタイ / Bow tie — neck — 60 — 16
    - scarf: マフラー / Scarf — neck — 100 — 20
    - bell: すず / Bell — neck — 60 — 16
    - medal: メダル / Medal — neck — 200 — 16
    - necklace: ネックレス / Necklace — neck — 150 — 16
  - Place ids in code: `head | face | neck`.
- **Wearing (spec 8.1).**
  - There are three places, each holding at most one item.
  - An owned item can be put on and taken off any number of times.
  - Worn items stay through evolution.
  - A new egg takes everything off, but the items stay owned. "A new egg" means restart, or startNewEgg after an ending.
  - The egg can't wear anything.
  - Wearing is refused for `notAlive`, `egg` and `asleep` (`outingRefusal`), then `notOwned`, then `wrongPlace`.
  - Taking an item off (`null`) is refused only by `outingRefusal`.
- **Drawing (spec 8.2).**
  - Accessories are drawn at 4×, the pet's pixel size.
  - Anchoring:
    - Each form has `head`, `face` and `neck` points in its 32 px art.
    - Each accessory has its own anchor in its art: head items at bottom-centre, face items at centre, neck items at top-centre. Task 3 measures each one from its opaque pixels.
    - On the stage, the accessory's anchor lands on the form's point: `stage x = 112 + 4·ax − 4·ox` and `stage y = 100 + 4·ay − 4·oy`.
  - The same points are used for every pose of a form.
  - Layer order: pet → neck → face → head.
  - The idle bounce and the care-effect `petDx` slide move the pet and its accessories together.
  - Accessories show only on the popup stage (main and もようがえ). They do not show in the zukan, on the roaming pet, in mini-games or in outings.
  - No night filter. Accessories look like the pet: the lights-off overlay covers both.
- **Buying.**
  - The same as furniture: `owned` and `noCoins` refusals, a confirm dialog first, and おみせ always open.
  - The bought note says where to go:
    - furniture → `shop_bought` 「もようがえで おけるよ」;
    - accessory → `shop_bought_acc` 「もようがえで つけられるよ」.
- **Balance (spec 5.2).**
  - Buying every furniture item and every accessory should take 20–30 generations of ordinary play. Task 8 states the model.
  - If the model lands outside 20–30, scale all furniture and accessory prices by one shared factor, rounded to tens, and record it. Leave rewards and sell prices unchanged.
- **Save format.**
  - Keep `version: 1`.
  - `home.ownedAcc`: drop unknown ids and repeats.
  - `home.worn`:
    - drop unknown places;
    - drop unknown items, items not owned, and items in the wrong place.
  - Parsing never fails the save, and unknown top-level `home` fields still pass through.
- **Popup.** The size stays 360×462, and the whole stage stays visible on もようがえ.
- **Repo rules.**
  - TDD: watch each test fail first.
  - Coverage stays about where it is.
  - Functions < 50 lines, files < 800 lines.
  - Immutable updates. No `console.log`.
  - UI text goes in both locales. Message JSON diffs only add lines, with Japanese left unescaped.
  - New controls get accessible names.
  - Do not run prettier.
- **PixelLab.**
  - Free quota only. Check `get_balance` first.
  - Stop and ask the owner if `generations_remaining` < 100.
  - This stage needs about 10 generations.
- **Commits.** Conventional format, **no attribution trailer**. Never push, open a PR or merge without the owner's say-so.
- **Language.** Talk to the owner in Japanese. Code, comments and test names stay in English.

## Review Focus

1. **A new egg.**
   - Wear a crown and a bow tie, then restart.
   - Expected: the new egg wears nothing, and both items are still owned and in the shop as owned.
   - Tested in Task 2 (`a new egg takes everything off and keeps it owned`).
2. **Evolution while dressed.**
   - Expected: the next form wears the same items, drawn at that form's own points.
   - Tested in Task 2 (`evolving keeps what is worn`) and Task 5 (`the stage draws worn items at the current form's points`).
3. **A stale or forged wear request.** The item is not owned, in the wrong place, worn by an egg, or worn while asleep.
   - Expected: refused with the right reason, and `home` unchanged.
   - Tested in Task 1 (`wear refusals in order`) and Task 2 (`wear requests that are not known ids are rejected`).
4. **The bought hint and a sync.**
   - The 60 s sync lands within 2.5 s of a buy.
   - Expected: the hint still hides on time.
   - Tested in Task 6 (App-level regression, carried from stage 3).
5. **Accessories during a care effect.**
   - Feeding slides the pet (`petDx`).
   - Expected: worn items slide with it and stay on the face.
   - Tested in Task 5 (`worn items move with the pet's slide and bounce`).
